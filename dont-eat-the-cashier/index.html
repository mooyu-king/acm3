Before your first run

Think of the encounter as an experiment: record the input, observe what the game actually shows and distinguish that result from what you expected.

A practical first attempt

Read each customer's request before choosing a tone. Keep an eye on which words in a reply are yours and which assumptions you have supplied. For a second shift, revisit one exchange with a different response.

What to watch on a first run

The shift starts with setup rather than a customer emergency. Take a moment to read the opening questions before clicking through. Once customers arrive, separate what they ask for from the threatening details of their appearance. The scary option and the rude option are not necessarily the same response.

A controlled second attempt

One useful second-run challenge is to keep the same attitude toward the customers for an entire shift. Try courtesy, caution or curiosity, then note the moment that attitude becomes difficult to maintain. This gives an alternate route a purpose beyond racing to the next ending.

After the opening scene

For a spoiler-light record, write down the customer you were dealing with and the reply you picked, but leave the ending itself undescribed. The next time you return you can test a different conversation without reading a summary of every surprise. Be mindful of mature material before putting on a walkthrough in a shared room.

Questions before playing

What kind of game is this?

Work a gas station shift where the customers are monsters and even an ordinary reply can take the conversation somewhere uncomfortable.

What should I try first?

Read each customer's request before choosing a tone. Keep an eye on which words in a reply are yours and which assumptions you have supplied. For a second shift, revisit one exchange with a different response.

What does the opening screen show?

The game asks about pronouns during setup. This screen comes before the customer interactions; it is not an order screen or a quick-time event.

Can I see the guide without starting the game?

Yes. The player only opens after you select Play. The illustrated notes below it do not require a game download.

Does refreshing restart the player?

Refreshing unloads the current frame. Do not use it as an undo button; use the game's own menus and save options where offered.

Why is there a loading screen after I press Play?

The cover belongs to this page; the game downloads its own resources after launch. Wait for its menu before pressing keys repeatedly.

How can I avoid ending spoilers?

Play before opening the optional video below. Footage may show later scenes or endings.
