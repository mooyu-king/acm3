Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 Beginner Guide

Quick answer

Leave the apartment → use the left staircase → open the Coffee Machine keyboard → type a supported word → collect the cup → keep it in Inventory → take it to a room or character, use it on Horu, or sell it through Computer → Shop.

01

Starting route

Go down the left staircase to find the Coffee Machine

After leaving Horu’s apartment, use the staircase on the left side of the corridor. The machine is in the lower shared area; the opening $10 is working capital for anomalous cups.

Official mechanic

HoruBrain’s official description defines the central loop as dropping in a coin, typing a word, and receiving a reality-bending drink.

02

04

Coffee effect loop

Type different coffee words, then drink each cup to reveal its effect

The machine turns supported words into different cups. Enter one coffee word, collect its cup, and let Horu drink it to test the result. Different inputs can change Horu’s form, appearance, dialogue, or event state.

Official direction

The creator describes discovering anomalous drinks, selling creations online, and expanding into more forms, combinations, and events. Exploration—not one fixed answer—is the intended loop.

Player strategy

Keep a short test log: word, target character, room, both current forms, and result. Change only one condition per attempt so a failed trigger still tells you something useful.

05

Return home, open Computer → Shop, and sell online

The Computer turns stored cups into repeatable experiment money. Keep enough capital for the next cup.

Observed sale · $4

Milk sold at $4 and increased the balance

The screenshots below form one continuous test: keep Milk in Inventory, list one unit for $4, and wait for the sold notification. The balance moves from $9 to $13 after the $4 sale.

Price comparison · $8 vs $30

Moderate pricing sold; a high listing stayed in stock during the test

A Coffee cup sold at $8 in the successful example. By contrast, a Coffee cup listed at $30 remained available during the observed interval. That is a comparison, not proof that $30 can never sell.

Tested on this browser build

$4 Milk and $8 Coffee sales completed in the captured runs. Use those values as reproducible starting points while you build a larger balance.

Prices are not a guaranteed formula

Sales timing can vary, and one screenshot cannot establish a permanent maximum. If a cup sits unsold, lower the price and compare again rather than spending all remaining coins.
