Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 Beginner Guide

Quick answer

Leave the apartment → use the left staircase → open the Coffee Machine keyboard → type a supported word → collect the cup → keep it in Inventory → take it to a room or character, use it on Horu, or sell it through Computer → Shop.

01

Starting route

Go down the left staircase to find the Coffee Machine

After leaving Horu’s apartment, use the staircase on the left side of the corridor. The machine is in the lower shared area; the opening $10 is working capital for anomalous cups.

Official mechanic

HoruBrain’s official description defines the central loop as dropping in a coin, typing a word, and receiving a reality-bending drink.

02

First cup

Spend 1 coin and try “milk”

Use a simple known example before spending the entire opening balance on guesses.

04

Exploration loop

Different words create cups for different experiments

The drink does not automatically teleport Horu to another room. First collect the cup, then choose where and on whom to test it. Some words produce a simple response, while others may change a form, dialogue, event, combination, or scene.

Official direction

The creator describes discovering anomalous drinks, selling creations online, and expanding into more forms, combinations, and events. Exploration—not one fixed answer—is the intended loop.

Player strategy

Keep a short test log: word, target character, room, both current forms, and result. Change only one condition per attempt so a failed trigger still tells you something useful.

05

Money loop

Return home, open Computer → Shop, and sell online

The Computer turns stored cups into repeatable experiment money. Keep enough capital for the next cup.

Community reported

Players on the official itch.io discussion recommend starting with Cup of Milk, returning home, opening Computer → Shop, and listing above the 1-coin cost.

Needs testing

Players report pricing quirks, including cups selling before adjustment and different results around 9 vs 10 coins. These are not official fixed price rules.
