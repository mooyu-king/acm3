Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 Beginner Guide

Quick answer

Start with $10 → leave the apartment → use the Coffee Machine → spend 1 coin → type “milk” → collect the cup → return home → Computer → Shop → list above cost → repeat.

01

Starting route

Use the $10 at the Coffee Machine

You do not need a normal coffee shop or ingredient store. The opening money is working capital for anomalous cups.

Official mechanic

The creator describes the central loop as inserting a coin, typing a word, and receiving a cup that enters Inventory.

02

First cup

Spend 1 coin and try “milk”

Use a simple known example before spending the entire opening balance on guesses.

Community reported

A community walkthrough uses Cup of Milk, returns home, opens Computer → Shop, and lists above the 1-coin cost.

Needs testing

Players report pricing quirks, including cups selling before adjustment and different results around 9 vs 10 coins. These are not official fixed price rules.
