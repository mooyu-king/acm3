Quick answer

Use the search box below for mechanically useful v0.1 words. Some form names are not direct inputs: AngelDemon returns Keyword not found and must be made with Heaven + Hell in the Mixer, while Pregnant returns Drink currently unavailable and is obtained by using Life.

01

All useful words

Search forms, ingredients, scenes, and utility words

This list focuses on words with a mapped gameplay purpose, not every dictionary term the input box may accept. Search by input word or expected result, then filter by the job you want the cup to perform.

AngelDemon — keyword not found

Do not type AngelDemon directly. Make separate Heaven and Hell cups, take them to Horu’s room, and combine them in the Mixer.

Pregnant — drink unavailable

Pregnant is the resulting form name, not an available direct drink in this build. Type Life to create the cup that produces the Pregnant form.

Death and Immortality — no direct transformation

Directly drinking either cup left Horu’s visible form unchanged. Five other tested words—acid, antimatter, blackhole, explosive, and knowledge—produce distinct fatal effects followed by the same Horu Died confirmation.

02

Verified hidden result · Mixer recipe

AngelDemon is made by mixing Heaven + Hell

AngelDemon is the output name, not a direct Coffee Machine keyword. First create and collect separate Heaven and Hell cups, keep both untouched, then place them together in the Mixer inside Horu’s room.

Heaven + Hell means Mixer

Create both cups, keep them in Inventory, and combine them. The September 17 captures confirm the output cup, Horu transformation, and scene routes for both character directions.

Ghost → Death means sequential drinks

An arrow means the same target drinks the cups one after another. Do not place Ghost and Death in the Mixer. This distinction is important when following scene routes.
