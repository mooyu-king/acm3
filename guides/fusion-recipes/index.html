Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 Fusion Recipes

Quick answer

Use Horu’s room Mixer for recipes marked with a plus sign. For routes marked with an arrow, skip the Mixer and give both cups to the same character in exact order. The current v0.1 mappings contain 16 Mixer recipes and 7 sequential transformation routes.
