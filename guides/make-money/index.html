How to Make Money in Anomalous Coffee Machine 3

Quick answer

The main money method is to spend 1 coin making a cup, keep it in Inventory, return home, open Computer → Shop, list the drink, and reinvest the sale. In captured tests, $4 Milk and $8 Coffee sold relatively quickly; a $30 Coffee listing remained unsold during the observed test period.

02

Observed pricing results

$4–$8 sold more easily; the $30 listing stayed in stock

Price changes affected the observed sale speed. The screenshots document successful low-to-moderate listings and an expensive listing that did not sell during the same test window. These are practical starting points, not a guaranteed permanent price formula.

Comparison · $8 vs $30

Moderate Coffee pricing moved; high pricing did not

The $8 example completed a Coffee sale. The $30 example kept its Coffee in stock for the observed interval, so an aggressive price can tie up inventory and delay reinvestment.

03

Advanced drink experiment

Test whether Mixer drinks support a higher selling price

Mixed drinks require two ingredient cups, so their cost and preparation time are higher. Create a valid fusion, keep the result in Inventory, then list identical mixed drinks at controlled prices to compare demand.

Confirmed baseline

$4 Milk and $8 Coffee completed sales in the captured browser tests. Use that range to establish cash flow before risking more ingredients.

Mixer pricing needs more evidence

The advanced-drink sale premium is an experiment, not a confirmed rule. Compare identical recipes and change only the price so the result remains useful.

04

Practical strategy

Use fast sales first, then test one variable at a time

Keep a cash reserve, begin around the tested $4–$8 range, and lower a listing that remains unsold. Once the balance is stable, compare direct cups with mixed drinks using the same observation period.
