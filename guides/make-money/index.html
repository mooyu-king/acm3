How to Make Money in Anomalous Coffee Machine 3

Quick answer

The main money method is to spend 1 coin making a cup, take the finished cup so it enters Inventory, return home, open Computer → Shop, list the drink, and reinvest the sale. Do not leave the machine before collecting it: the $1 is spent and the cup is not added automatically. In captured tests, $4 Milk and $8 Coffee sold relatively quickly; a $30 Coffee listing remained unsold during the observed test period.

Make a cup, list it in the Computer Shop, then reinvest

Selling stored coffee is the most direct repeatable way to build funds in the current browser build. Protect at least one coin for the next cup so a slow listing does not stop your run.

Tested warning · $1 is not refunded

Take the finished cup before leaving the Coffee Machine

Producing one Coffee charges $1, but the cup stays in the machine until you click or tap it. If you walk away without collecting it, the drink is not moved into Inventory automatically and that dollar has been wasted. Always confirm the cup appears in Inventory before returning home to sell it.
