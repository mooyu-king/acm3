18+ restricted guideThis page explains adult game mechanics and keeps explicit scene artwork hidden until age confirmation.
Quick answer

Prepare a transformation cup and a separate Sex cup. Blood, Heaven, Hell, and Ghost produced captured scenes in our v0.1 tests. The same Horu Hunger + human Neighbor + Sex workflow did not trigger a scene, so Hunger is documented as a negative test rather than an unlock.

Troubleshooting

Why did nothing happen?

Check the conditions before assuming the browser build is broken.

01The form may not have a scene+

The current game is still in development. Some transformations exist without a confirmed animated scene.

02The character-state combination may be wrong+

Re-check who is transformed and who remains human. Do not rely on appearance alone; use the Gallery or state UI when possible.

03You transformed a character but skipped the trigger cup+

Transformation and scene triggering are separate actions in the reported rule. Prepare and use the trigger cup after the states are ready.

04Your version may differ+

Future itch, Steam, and browser updates may change available scenes or conditions. Record the build before reporting a route as verified.

05Milk did not prepare a scene-compatible form+

Milk is useful as a safe baseline and economic starter cup, but the tested milk → Sex Cup → room 702 route only continued normal dialogue. Try a confirmed transformation first, verify the character state, then test the trigger cup again.
