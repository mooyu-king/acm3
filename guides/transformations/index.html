All Transformations in Anomalous Coffee Machine 3

Quick answer

The game Gallery screenshots contain 78 numbered form entries: 55 direct forms, 16 Mixer forms, and 7 exact-order forms. The database now follows that same #1–#78 sequence and shows the exact word or recipe beside each slot. Direct Lava is retained as a separate tested result because typing Lava alone does not unlock a visible Gallery transformation.
