All Transformations in Anomalous Coffee Machine 3

Quick answer

The v0.1 Gallery contains 80 form entries. Use single words for 55 direct forms, the Mixer for 16 fused forms, and exact-order drinks for 7 sequential forms. Coffee and Horny are present in the Gallery data, but their direct unlock routes are not mapped in the current synergy table and remain marked for testing.

02

Three unlock systems

Match the form to the correct interaction

The same two words can behave differently depending on whether they are mixed into a new cup or given one after another. Check the method label before spending coins.

55

Single-cup forms

Type the listed word, collect the cup, then use it on Horu or give it to the room 702 Neighbor.

Search direct words →
16

Mixer forms

Create two ingredient cups, keep both in Inventory, and combine them with the Mixer in Horu’s room.

Open Mixer recipes →
7

Sequential forms

Give both cups to the same character in order. Ghost → Death and Immortality → Lava are examples.

Open sequence routes →
03

Controlled testing

Verify one state change at a time

Record the target character, starting form, cup or recipe, final form, and Gallery change. Reset with Cure before testing a route that expects the Normal starting state. This prevents a previous transformation from changing the next result.
