What is Anomalous Coffee Machine 3?

Before the incidents, Horu was just trying to make rent.

In this sequel, Horu is a work-from-home game artist who discovers an outdated, reality-bending coffee machine in the liminal stairwell between the 12th and 13th floors of her apartment building. Insert a coin, type a word, and the machine dispenses a drink that should not exist.

Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 expands the familiar word-and-cup experiment into a broader simulation and management loop. Keep each cup in your inventory, drink it, offer it to a neighbor, or sell it online. Discover forms and combinations, attract followers, earn money, meet characters, and spread anomalous coffee across the city.

This page lets adults aged 18 and over play the free v0.1 online browser demo. The game remains in development, while a separate paid full version may contain additional content. Public artwork on this site stays non-explicit.

Browser guide · 07

Browser requirements for the free online demo

The itch.io release lists Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 as a free HTML5 game for browsers, alongside Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android downloads. It is an in-development 2D interactive fiction title made with Godot, with keyboard, mouse, touchscreen, smartphone input, English text, and subtitles.

This website presents the free v0.1 browser demo from the supplied Windows package. The restored build uses Godot 4.5.1, the GL Compatibility renderer, and a 1280 by 720 viewport. Because the game data is large, it begins loading only after you pass the 18+ confirmation.

The basic loop is simple: type a supported word and receive a cup. The sequel adds inventory choices, neighbors, online selling, social-media growth, money, events, forms, and combinations around that central experiment.

Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 is strictly for adults aged 18 and over. The creator warns that it includes sex scenes, disturbing imagery, brutal gore, horror, and other mature depictions. Public-facing images on this page deliberately avoid explicit scenes. Do not enter if you are under 18, adult content is illegal in your location, or these themes are unsuitable for you.

If the game remains blank, allow JavaScript and WebAssembly, disable blockers for the game host, and try a current Chrome, Edge, or Firefox browser. Audio may wait for your first click. Landscape can work on a larger mobile device, but desktop remains recommended for text and controls.
