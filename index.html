First Coffee

Use the starting money at the anomalous Coffee Machine and keep your first cup as a resource.

  1. Start a New Game and check the $10 balance.
  2. Leave Horu’s apartment instead of looking for a shop inside the room.
  3. Use the staircase on the left side of the corridor to reach the Coffee Machine.
  4. Click the keyboard panel in the center of the Coffee Machine; the full input keyboard then slides into view.
  5. Enter a simple word such as “milk” and submit it; a recognized drink costs 1 coin.
  6. Click the lower dispenser, collect the cup, and confirm that it appears in Inventory.
  7. Take the cup to a room or character, use it on Horu, keep it for later, or sell it through Computer → Shop.
  8. Keep the first cup so you can learn the selling loop.

What is Anomalous Coffee Machine 3?

Before the incidents, Horu was just trying to make rent.

In this sequel, Horu is a work-from-home game artist who discovers an outdated, reality-bending coffee machine in the liminal stairwell between the 12th and 13th floors of her apartment building. Insert a coin, type a word, and the machine dispenses a drink that should not exist.

Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 expands the familiar word-and-cup experiment into a broader simulation and management loop. Keep each cup in your inventory, drink it, offer it to a neighbor, or sell it online. Discover forms and combinations, attract followers, earn money, meet characters, and spread anomalous coffee across the city.

This page lets adults aged 18 and over play the free v0.1 online browser demo. The game remains in development, while a separate paid full version may contain additional content. Public artwork on this site stays non-explicit.

Browser guide · 07

Browser requirements for the free online demo

The itch.io release lists Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 as a free HTML5 game for browsers, alongside Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android downloads. It is an in-development 2D interactive fiction title made with Godot, with keyboard, mouse, touchscreen, smartphone input, English text, and subtitles.

This website presents the free v0.1 browser demo from the supplied Windows package. The restored build uses Godot 4.5.1, the GL Compatibility renderer, and a 1280 by 720 viewport. Because the game data is large, it begins loading only after you pass the 18+ confirmation.

The basic loop is simple: type a supported word and receive a cup. The sequel adds inventory choices, neighbors, online selling, social-media growth, money, events, forms, and combinations around that central experiment.

Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 is strictly for adults aged 18 and over. The creator warns that it includes sex scenes, disturbing imagery, brutal gore, horror, and other mature depictions. Public-facing images on this page deliberately avoid explicit scenes. Do not enter if you are under 18, adult content is illegal in your location, or these themes are unsuitable for you.

If the game remains blank, allow JavaScript and WebAssembly, disable blockers for the game host, and try a current Chrome, Edge, or Firefox browser. Audio may wait for your first click. Landscape can work on a larger mobile device, but desktop remains recommended for text and controls.

01Is Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 suitable for minors?+

No. The game contains explicit sexual material, nudity, disturbing imagery, brutal gore, horror, and transformation content. You must be at least 18 years old and legally permitted to view adult content where you live.

02Can I play Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 online for free?+

Yes. This page provides the free v0.1 browser demo supplied from the game's free HTML5 release. A separate paid full version may offer different or additional content.

03What kind of game is it?+

It is a 2D interactive fiction, simulation, visual novel, and light management game. Insert a coin, type a word, collect the anomalous drink, then drink it, offer it to a neighbor, or sell it online.

04Who created the original game?+

Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 was created by HoruBrain. This browser portal is an unofficial presentation of the supplied Godot build.

05Does the browser version save progress?+

Godot web games generally store progress in browser-managed storage. Use the same browser profile, allow site storage, and avoid private browsing if you want progress to persist.

06Why does the game take time to start?+

The browser downloads and compiles a WebAssembly engine plus a large game data pack. Keep the tab open during the first load; later visits may be faster when caching is available.

07Can I play on a phone?+

The original page lists touchscreen and smartphone input. Landscape orientation is recommended, though a desktop browser remains the most comfortable option for text and controls.

08How do keywords work in Anomalous Coffee Machine 3?+

Insert a coin and type a word into the machine. Recognized words produce anomalous drinks with different results. Try concrete objects, emotions, concepts, and unusual ideas, then decide whether to keep, drink, share, or sell each cup.

09Do I need to download or install the game?+

No installation is needed for the free browser demo on this page. Confirm that you are 18 or older, wait for the WebAssembly game files to load, and play inside the browser frame.

10Is the full version of Anomalous Coffee Machine 3 free?+

This page offers the free v0.1 web demo. The creator also distributes a separate paid full version, so content, updates, and availability can differ from this browser build.

11Which platforms support Anomalous Coffee Machine 3?+

The creator lists HTML5 browsers, Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android. This site hosts the HTML5 browser demo; use the official HoruBrain or itch.io pages for the creator's other releases.

12Where can I get help without closing the game?+
