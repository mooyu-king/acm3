Privacy Policy

Information you provide

You do not need an account to view the public guide previews or play the browser game. If you create a free guide account, we process your email address, authentication identifiers, registration and login timestamps, IP address, user agent, and security information needed to maintain the account, investigate abuse, and protect the service. We do not request payment information or identity documents for an ACM3 guide account. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to include in that message.

Guide accounts

Account authentication is provided by Supabase. Supabase processes account and session information and maintains authentication audit logs for events such as registration, sign-in, token refresh, and sign-out. Those logs can include timestamps, IP address, and user-agent information. Access to the site's account administration view is restricted to the site owner and is used for account support, security, and abuse prevention. Authentication tokens are stored in browser-managed storage so you can remain signed in. Google sign-in sends the minimum account information required for authentication between Google and Supabase. ACM3 accounts are separate from accounts used by other websites. Authentication records are retained only as reasonably necessary for these purposes and applicable legal obligations.

Technical delivery data

Hosting and security providers may process standard technical data needed to deliver and protect the website, such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamps, performance information, and error logs.

Analytics

This site loads an analytics script from data.1back.link. The service may process page requests and limited technical information to measure traffic and site performance. The script is configured for anomalouscoffeemachine3.com and sends analytics requests to https://data.1back.link/api/send. The separately hosted browser game sends privacy-friendly pageview and embed-access events to the Plausible Events API using the site identifier s.anomalouscoffeemachine3.com. These events may include the referring parent origin so unauthorized iframe embedding can be detected.

Third-party advertising

This site currently uses popunder advertising delivered by third-party advertising providers, including the Adsterra network domain profitableratecpmnetwork.com. The advertising script is enabled on desktop and mobile devices and is included near the end of the page HTML without waiting for client-side initialization, a timed delay, or scrolling. Advertising is not loaded on the About Us and Contact pages. Advertising providers may process IP address, browser and device information, referral data, ad interactions, cookies, or similar identifiers to deliver, limit, measure, and protect advertising. An advertisement or interaction may open its destination in a new tab or window. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and consent requirements.

Age confirmation and browser storage

The current age confirmation is held only for the active page session and is not used as identity verification. The embedded Godot game may use browser-managed storage for settings and progress. That data normally remains associated with the game host in your browser. Clearing site data, blocking storage, or using private browsing may erase or prevent saves.
