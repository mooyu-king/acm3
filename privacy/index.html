Privacy Policy

Information you provide

You do not need an account to use this website, and the site does not ask for your name, address, payment information, or identity documents. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to include in that message.

Technical delivery data

Hosting and security providers may process standard technical data needed to deliver and protect the website, such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamps, performance information, and error logs.

Analytics

This site loads an analytics script from data.1back.link. The service may process page requests and limited technical information to measure traffic and site performance. The script is configured for anomalouscoffeemachine3.com and sends analytics requests to https://data.1back.link/api/send.

Third-party advertising

This site displays advertising delivered by third-party advertising providers, including Adsterra network domains such as profitableratecpmnetwork.com and highrevenueformat.com. The site uses footer native and banner placements, plus a social-bar placement limited to desktop-class devices. It does not intentionally load a popunder placement. Advertising providers may process IP address, browser and device information, referral data, ad interactions, cookies, or similar identifiers to deliver, limit, measure, and protect advertising. Clicking an advertisement may open its destination in a new tab or window. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and consent requirements.

Age confirmation and browser storage

The current age confirmation is held only for the active page session and is not used as identity verification. The embedded Godot game may use browser-managed storage for settings and progress. That data normally remains associated with the game host in your browser. Clearing site data, blocking storage, or using private browsing may erase or prevent saves.

Embedded game and YouTube

The game is delivered from a separate game-host subdomain and is not loaded until you confirm that you are 18 or older. This page also embeds videos using YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain. Loading or playing a video can still send technical information to Google or YouTube under their own policies.

Your choices

You can decline the age confirmation, avoid playing videos, adjust browser cookie or tracking protections, block third-party scripts, clear stored data, or stop using the site. Blocking advertising scripts may leave an empty advertisement area. For privacy questions, email mooyuking@gmail.com.
